PART I

The Uniqueness of the Method of Sentence Resolution

The exact locus of originality: not isolated ingredients without ancestry, but the governing object, the factorization, the complete generated matrix, and the disciplined direction by which a sentence earns its address.

The strongest account of the method’s uniqueness does not begin by pretending that nothing similar has ever existed. That would weaken the claim before the method had even been properly distinguished. The public history of semantics, pragmatics, discourse theory, event structure, speech-act classification, dialogue annotation, and multidimensional conceptual representation contains real relatives. Some of them approach one portion of the method very closely. Dynamic semantics comes close to the movement from an input condition to an output condition. Questions Under Discussion come close to the idea of an unresolved pressure that an utterance answers. Speech-act theory comes close to the distinction between what a sentence says and what saying it accomplishes. Event structure comes close to movement, result, state, and efficacy. Factorial taxonomies come close to the architectural principle by which independent axes generate a finite field. Conceptual spaces come close to the idea that meaning may have an address.

None of this diminishes the method. It reveals where the method must be located. Its originality does not lie in having independently invented transformation, openness, accomplishment, result, multidimensionality, or persistence. Its originality lies in making these into one operational object: the completed intelligibility of a sentence, reconstructed as an exact passage from an unsettled condition through an answering movement into a completed landing, then resolved by three independent dimensions and carried forward as a survivor.

The method is distinctive not because every ingredient is new, but because it places familiar ingredients under a new governing object and forces them through one complete, auditable machine.

1. The governing object is different

The first distinction is the most important. Existing theories usually begin by deciding what kind of thing they are studying. A truth-conditional theory studies the conditions under which a proposition is true. Dynamic semantics studies the transformation of an information state or discourse context. Speech-act theory studies the communicative act performed by a speaker. Event semantics studies eventualities, predicates, aspectual structure, or results. Dialogue-act annotation studies communicative functions within interaction. Conceptual spaces study positions within dimensions of quality or similarity.

The Method of Sentence Resolution begins elsewhere. Its governing object is completed intelligibility. This is neither the proposition alone nor the social act alone. It is the complete structural event by which a sentence ceases to remain open in one exact respect and becomes available as something another act of understanding can inherit.

This shift matters because it changes what counts as evidence. The method does not ask only what information has been added, what act the speaker intends, what event the verb denotes, or what discourse function the utterance serves. It asks what exact insufficiency was present before the sentence landed, what the sentence did to that insufficiency, what completed result now stands, how that result was structurally constituted, and in what form it remains available afterward.

That object is wider than a discourse update but narrower than a general philosophy of meaning. It is wider because the unsettled condition may be an absent distinction, unavailable path, unspecified bearing, defective arrangement, unorganized plurality, or outstanding demand. It is narrower because the method refuses to claim everything about the sentence. It recovers the particular completion the sentence itself achieves and withholds classification where that completion cannot be positively established.

2. Dynamic semantics supplies transformation, but not the kind of completion

Dynamic and update semantics are the closest relatives to the basic X→Y architecture. They reject the idea that a sentence should be represented only by a static truth condition. A sentence can instead be treated as an operation on a context: it accepts an input information state and produces an output information state. This is genuinely near the method’s central movement. It also comes close to inheritance, because the updated context constrains what later discourse can presuppose, introduce, retrieve, or continue.

Yet the resemblance becomes a distinction at exactly the point where the method becomes itself. Dynamic semantics specifies that an update occurs and models what information or discourse structure changes. The Method of Sentence Resolution asks what kind of answering accomplishment the sentence performs. Was a difference made operative? Was further availability opened? Was a bearing established? Was a failure repaired? Were parts organized into a whole? Was a live demand discharged?

Dynamic semantics: how a context changes.
Sentence Resolution: what irreducible form of completion the change accomplishes, how the landing is constituted, and how the result survives.

This is why Work is not reducible to update. All six Works are updates in a broad sense, but they are not the same update. Distinction, Expansion, Relation, Correction, Integration, and Rest are intended to be irreducible accomplishments. An update account may represent the resulting context perfectly while remaining silent about whether the transformation made a difference active, increased reach, established a dependency, repaired a misfit, constituted a whole, or discharged a demand.

3. Questions Under Discussion supply openness, but only one species of it

Questions Under Discussion theory offers another close relative. In that tradition, discourse is organized by open questions and live alternatives. An utterance is relevant because it addresses what remains unresolved. This resembles X-pressure and is especially close to Rest, where a live demand for further uptake is discharged.

But the method generalizes the form of openness beyond inquiry. X is not merely a question waiting for an answer. X is the exact condition in which something is not yet structurally available. Two candidates may be present without a usable distinction. A source may exist without a possible branch. Terms may be present without an established bearing. A configuration may exist while violating a criterion. Parts may coexist without whole-dependent roles. Alternatives may remain live and continue to demand settlement.

QUD theory is therefore a strong ancestor of one part of the architecture: discourse organized by unresolved pressure. The Method of Sentence Resolution turns that insight into a sixfold ontology of incompletion. It asks not only which question is open, but what kind of absence, failure, non-belonging, restricted reach, unestablished bearing, or uncompleted demand makes the sentence necessary.

4. Speech-act theory supplies accomplishment, but classifies a different act

Speech-act theory is close to the method’s refusal to collapse meaning into content. It insists that saying something is also doing something. An utterance may assert, request, promise, declare, express, warn, or direct. Searle’s use of multiple dimensions also provides an important methodological precedent: complex utterance types can be distinguished by independent structural criteria rather than by a loose list of examples.

The difference lies in the target. Speech-act theory ordinarily classifies the speaker’s communicative action. The Method of Sentence Resolution classifies the structural accomplishment within the completed intelligibility itself. A speaker may assert a distinction, ask for a correction, promise an integration, or declare a Rest. Illocutionary type and Work do not coincide.

This difference can be stated precisely:

speech-act classification:
What interpersonal act is the speaker performing?

sentence-resolution classification:
What structural incompletion is the sentence resolving?

The method therefore preserves the speech-act insight while redirecting it. “What saying accomplishes” remains central, but accomplishment is recovered from X→movement→Y rather than from communicative force alone. The same illocutionary force can carry different Works, and the same Work can appear inside different illocutionary forces.

5. Factorial taxonomies supply the architecture, but not the ontology

The strongest precedent against claiming novelty for the mere use of independent axes is Stiles’s factorial taxonomy of intersubjective illocutionary acts. Its importance should be acknowledged without hesitation. It demonstrates that utterances can be located in a finite multidimensional space generated by independent distinctions. It also shows that form and intent can be separated and recombined into a larger grid.

What it does not supply is the ontology of the present lattice. Its dimensions concern interpersonal position, viewpoint, and presumed knowledge. Its cells classify modes such as Disclosure, Advisement, Question, Interpretation, and Acknowledgment. It does not reconstruct X, does not identify an answering movement, does not recover Y, does not distinguish the topology of the landing, and does not identify the mode in which the result survives.

The existence of Stiles’s model therefore sharpens, rather than dissolves, the originality claim. The claim cannot be that no one has ever multiplied independent dimensions to classify utterances. The claim must be that these particular dimensions are novel in conjunction because they are derived from a different object.

Architectural precedent: independent axes can generate a finite taxonomy.
Distinctive contribution: Work, Orientation, and Survivor are axes of completed intelligibility, not axes of interpersonal response mode.

6. Event structure supplies movement and result, but not sentence-wide resolution

Event-structure and result-semantics traditions come close to the method’s distinction among movement, landing, standing result, and accomplished effect. They have long examined how an event produces a state or location and how predicates encode activities, accomplishments, achievements, and results. This is substantial ancestry. It shows that completion and resulting condition are not foreign to linguistic analysis.

Yet event structure usually begins with verbs, predicates, eventualities, and argument realization. The Method of Sentence Resolution begins with one complete sentence and asks what completed intelligibility it contributes, including sentences whose decisive event is not naturally captured as a lexical resultative. Identity, comparison, option, ordering, recurrence, repair, organization, retrospective settlement, and continuation discharge can all be structurally resolved even when no single result verb carries the analysis.

The Survivor axis also differs from a simple result-state distinction. Is identifies a standing determination. With identifies a result whose constitutive bearing must remain present. Does identifies accomplished efficacy: an effect, permission, prevention, capacity, transformation, or cessation that remains operatively available. These are not merely aspectual classes. They state how the completed achievement persists for later uptake.

7. Multidimensional dialogue annotation supplies operational caution

Dialogue-act annotation standards provide a close precedent for two practical commitments: a single linguistic unit may carry more than one function, and communicative contribution may need to be represented in several dimensions at once. This supports the method’s refusal to force every grammatical sentence into a single coordinate. A compound sentence may contain several completed settlements, each requiring its own X, movement, Y, and coordinate.

But once again, the object differs. Dialogue annotation classifies functions within interaction: feedback, turn management, information seeking, social obligation, and related communicative actions. The Method of Sentence Resolution classifies local structures of completion. It may be used on dialogue, but it is not restricted to dialogue and does not derive its axes from conversational management.

8. Conceptual spaces supply addressability, but not accomplishment coordinates

Conceptual-spaces theory provides a broad precedent for representing meaning in multidimensional form. Concepts may occupy regions defined by quality dimensions, and distances may model similarity. This makes the general notion of a semantic address less unusual.

The present coordinates, however, are not measurements of semantic content. They do not say where “red,” “heavy,” “bird,” or “justice” lies within a quality space. Nor do they place sentences near one another because their topics are similar. Two sentences about entirely different domains may share a coordinate because they complete the same structural accomplishment. Conversely, two sentences about the same object may occupy different cells because they make the object intelligible through different Works, Orientations, or Survivors.

9. The three axes are not decorative labels

The method’s most substantial originality lies in the factorization itself. Work, Orientation, and Survivor are not three descriptive headings attached after interpretation. Each answers a different question and must be recovered independently.

Work asks what answering accomplishment transforms X into Y. It is productive and irreducible. Orientation asks what structural disposition makes Y complete: one governing locus, an irreducibly plural span, or release from prior governance. Survivor asks in what form the completed achievement remains available: standing determination, constitutive co-presence, or accomplished efficacy.

Work:
What was done?

Orientation:
How was the landing constituted?

Survivor:
How does the result remain available?

This separation is conceptually demanding. A relation may settle Toward, Across, or Away. Any of those may survive as Is, With, or Does. The axes prevent one intuitive feature from swallowing the others. A sentence is not placed in “Relation” and left there. It must also be shown whether one pivot governs the relation, whether the relation exists across indispensable positions, or whether the relation is revealed through release from prior surface governance. Then the analyst must show whether the completed relation stands, remains with its constitutive bearing, or persists as an accomplished effect.

10. The recovery direction is part of the theory

Many classification systems provide categories and instructions for assigning examples. The Method of Sentence Resolution builds a stronger asymmetry into the procedure: the cell must never govern discovery. The sentence must be recovered before the table is consulted for confirmation.

This order is not an administrative convenience. It is an epistemic safeguard. It prevents the analyst from seeing a familiar word, choosing a cell, and rewriting the sentence until it fits. It also distinguishes the method from taxonomies that begin with a codebook and search for the nearest label.

The cell name is introduced late and functions only as a compression handle. “Sequential Link,” “Focal Adjustment,” or “Continuation Discharge” cannot serve as the original reason for classification. The reason must be a reconstructed event. This makes the method auditable: another reader can inspect the sentence, the exhausted saying, X, the movement, Y, and each axis independently.

The permission to withhold is equally important. A theory that claims a complete written matrix does not thereby prove that every sentence is recoverable or uniquely assignable. The method protects itself by distinguishing formal discreteness from empirical exhaustiveness. If X, Y, the movement, an axis, translation fidelity, or context repair remains unsupported, the coordinate is withheld.

11. Multilingual realization is not decoration

The ten-language stage gives the method another distinctive feature. Translation is not used to display universality after the coordinate has been chosen. It is used before structural classification as controlled variation. One candidate intelligibility is expressed across different syntactic, morphological, aspectual, and idiomatic resources. The analysis then asks what every faithful realization must preserve.

This does not prove language-independent universality by itself. It does, however, reduce the accidental authority of one surface form. It also exposes translation strain, ambiguity, changes in aspect, altered modality, shifted negation, and other failures that could make an English-only analysis appear firmer than it is.

12. Survivor and inheritance complete the machine

The concept of a survivor is perhaps the clearest point at which the method exceeds ordinary classification. A sentence does not merely receive a coordinate and disappear. Its completed result remains available. The method distinguishes Y, the full local landing, from the portable survivor that can constrain later reasoning, memory, action, comparison, correction, disagreement, or sentence processing.

This gives formal shape to inheritance pressure. A later sentence may depend upon an earlier survivor by using an object it established, a distinction it fixed, a relation it licensed, a possibility it opened, a condition it supplied, or an interpretation it excluded. The first sentence has finished, yet its completion remains structurally active.

13. Where the originality claim should be placed

The method should not claim to have invented every intuition it uses. It should claim originality at the level of organized conjunction.

The public relatives show that transformation, unresolved pressure, accomplishment, result, multidimensional classification, discourse persistence, and semantic addressability all have histories. No single relative, however, appears to supply the following machine as one theory:

completed intelligibility as the governing object; explicit recovery of X, answering movement, and Y; six accomplishment families; a topology axis of Toward, Across, and Away; a persistence axis of Is, With, and Does; the complete 6×3×3 matrix; individual governing specifications for every coordinate; delayed full-cell confirmation; multilingual semantic exhaustion before classification; permission to withhold; compound-settlement analysis; and a portable survivor capable of exerting inheritance pressure.

The plausible originality lies in the exact ontology, the independence of the three axes, the complete generated matrix, and the direction of recovery by which a sentence earns its coordinate.

14. The final distinction

The nearest theories can each be described by the part of the method they illuminate. Dynamic semantics illuminates transformation. QUD theory illuminates unresolved demand. Speech-act theory illuminates accomplishment. Event semantics illuminates movement and result. Factorial taxonomies illuminate multidimensional generation. Dialogue annotation illuminates compound contribution. Conceptual spaces illuminate addressability. Discourse theory illuminates persistence.

The Method of Sentence Resolution is the point at which all of these pressures are reorganized around a different question:

What exact completion has this sentence achieved,
how was that completion constituted,
and what remains available because it landed?

That question generates the entire method. It requires a semantically exhausted saying before classification. It requires the unsettled pressure to be named. It requires the answering movement to be stated as an operation. It requires the landing to be distinguished from its consequences. It requires the accomplishment, topology, and persistence of the landing to be independently recovered. It requires the final coordinate to be confirmed against a governing cell rather than guessed from verbal resemblance. It requires the result to survive language-by-language checking. And it allows the analysis to stop honestly where recoverability stops.

The method is therefore neither an isolated invention without precedent nor a mere rearrangement of existing theories. It is a new synthesis with a specific centre. Its relatives explain why parts of it feel intellectually familiar. Its architecture explains why the whole does not. The originality is not that language changes contexts, answers questions, performs acts, describes events, occupies dimensions, or constrains later discourse. The originality is that one completed sentence is treated as a reconstructable settlement event whose Work, Orientation, and Survivor together yield one formal address in a complete lattice of intelligibility—and that this address must be earned by a disciplined passage from linguistic surface to invariant completion.

PART I

The Periodic Table

The governing claim, the three dimensions, and the complete fifty-four-coordinate matrix of completed intelligibility.

1. The claim

Every completed sentence has both its ordinary meaning and a formal address in a three-dimensional lattice of completed intelligibility.

A sentence is resolved according to three independent coordinates:

  1. Work — the kind of answering accomplishment performed;
  2. Orientation — the structural disposition under which that accomplishment reaches completion;
  3. Survivor — the formal manner in which the completed achievement remains available.

The address of a sentence is therefore:

Sentence → (Work, Orientation, Survivor)

The dimensions contain six, three, and three admissible values respectively:

6 × 3 × 3 = 54

Equivalently:

6 choose 1 × 3 choose 1 × 3 choose 1 = 54

Each sentence retains its particular meaning while also occupying one of the fifty-four positions. The lattice does not replace meaning. It specifies the structural form of the sentence's completed intelligibility.

Completed intelligibility is an X→Y settlement. Every completion is specified by:

  1. the Work performed in transforming X into Y;
  2. the Orientation by which Y settles;
  3. the Survivor-mode in which the settlement persists.

Work × Orientation × Survivor = one completion coordinate.

2. Work

Definition

Work is the irreducible kind of answering change by which something structurally open becomes differently available and capable of completion.

A sentence may contain several Works. Its governing Work is the Work whose accomplishment cannot be removed without changing the kind of completion the sentence achieves.

Work is productive. It says what was done, not merely where the doing was organised or what remained afterward.

Distinction

Distinction makes a difference structurally operative.

After Distinction, something can be picked out, separated, bounded, opposed, graded, sorted, or excluded in a way that was not previously active.

Its minimum achievement is not merely noticing that two things differ. The difference becomes usable within the completed intelligibility.

Governing question: What difference has been made active?

Expansion

Expansion increases or releases availability beyond an earlier restriction.

After Expansion, something can begin from a source, continue, branch, pass through, reach across, spread throughout, become optional, remain traversable, or open beyond a former edge.

Expansion is not merely physical enlargement. Its defining achievement is that more continuation, reach, access, option, or availability now exists.

Governing question: What has become newly available beyond the earlier limit?

Relation

Relation makes one distinct availability intelligible through its bearing upon another.

After Relation, terms may be directed, balanced, sequenced, answered, reciprocally adjusted, cycled, aligned, corresponded, or made jointly productive of a pattern.

Relation does not require its terms to become one whole. They remain distinct while one becomes intelligible through another.

Governing question: What now bears upon what?

Correction

Correction changes a configuration because it fails against a requirement, standard, limit, condition, or needed bearing.

After Correction, something has been adjusted, reweighted, redirected, tested, reversed, reset, or released from a fault.

Correction requires more than change. The change answers an inadequacy, discrepancy, misdirection, disorder, or failure.

Governing question: What failure has been answered, and in what respect has it been set right?

Integration

Integration makes distinct contributions belong together as one organised arrangement, whole, equilibrium, or operative capacity.

After Integration, the contributions are not merely related. Their belonging together constitutes something that none of them supplies alone.

Integration can produce an object, an array, a stable interlock, a system in operation, or a unified act.

Governing question: What organised belonging now exists that did not exist before?

Rest

Rest settles an outstanding openness so that no further answer of the relevant kind is structurally owed.

The openness may concern a choice, identity, path, recurrence, condition, case, transformation, interpretation, or demand for continuation.

Rest is not merely stopping. Something can stop accidentally while remaining unresolved. Rest occurs when the open requirement itself has been settled.

Governing question: What no longer asks to remain open?

This preserves the distinction between:

  • terminal closure, where another Work reaches structural sufficiency;
  • governing Rest, where settlement itself is the sentence's decisive achievement.

3. Orientation

Definition

Orientation is the structural disposition under which the governing Work reaches completion.

Orientation does not say what kind of Work occurs. It says how the decisive accomplishment is organised relative to the field from which it arises.

It is not necessarily literal spatial direction.

Toward

Toward is completion organised by a privileged locus.

The decisive Work originates from, converges upon, is judged by, or is concentrated around one source, target, pivot, standard, whole, choice, or endpoint.

Several elements may participate, but one locus governs their completed disposition.

Recognition question: Can the decisive organisation be understood through one governing locus?

Across

Across is completion organised through an irreducibly plural span.

The accomplishment depends upon several positions, stages, cases, roles, correspondences, or mutually bearing contributions. No single locus can replace the distributed support without changing the completion.

Across does not merely mean that many things are mentioned. The plurality is constitutive.

Recognition question: Must the completion be supported across more than one indispensable position?

Away

Away is completion organised by departure from prior governance.

Something that previously constrained, contained, directed, identified, obstructed, or demanded the process is removed, reversed, exceeded, released, replaced, or left behind.

Away is therefore more transitional than Toward or Across. It concerns a change in what is allowed to govern the result.

Recognition question: What former condition ceases to govern the completed result?

4. Survivor

Definition

Survivor is the formal manner in which the completed achievement remains available after the governing Work has finished.

It is not everything the sentence means. It is the aspect of completion that cannot be surrendered without changing the kind of result.

Survivor answers:

What form of completed intelligibility is carried forward?

Is

Is survives as a standing determination.

What remains can be represented as a state, identity, placement, distinction, presence, arrangement, or settled configuration.

The history that produced it may recede without destroying the result.

Recognition question: Can the completion now stand as what is so?

With

With survives as constitutive co-presence.

What remains cannot be represented adequately as one isolated state. A contrast, counterpart, condition, frame, balance, relation, pairing, or mutual dependence must remain with it.

With does not mean that several things merely happened to be involved. Their co-belonging remains part of the result.

Recognition question: What must remain together for the completion still to be what it is?

Does

Does survives as accomplished efficacy.

What remains is an event, passage, response, transformation, discharge, operation, or capacity: something having happened, been completed, become possible, or become able to operate.

Does need not mean ongoing motion. A completed act or an acquired capacity may survive as Does.

Recognition question: What accomplished doing or ability cannot be reduced to a standing state or retained pairing?

PART II

The Method of Sentence Resolution

The operational procedure by which a sentence’s completed intelligibility is recovered, resolved, composed, confirmed, and carried forward.

Operational sequence at a glance

  1. 1 · Establish the objectSelect the active sentence and secure faithful multilingual realizations.
  2. 2 · Exhaust the sayingComplete the ordinary semantic account before structural terminology enters.
  3. 3 · Recover the eventName X, the answering movement, and Y.
  4. 4 · Resolve the axesRecover Work, Orientation, and Survivor independently.
  5. 5 · Compose the addressForm the coordinate only after the three dimensions are supported.
  6. 6 · Confirm the full cellTest the recovered structure against the governing specification.
  7. 7 · Converge or withholdCheck language-by-language stability and withhold where evidence fails.
  8. 8 · Carry forwardRecover the survivor and trace inheritance beyond the sentence.

1. The governing aim

The method does not begin by asking which of the 54 coordinates a sentence resembles. It begins by recovering the completed intelligibility carried by the sentence.

The governing object is:

completed intelligibility
=
X→Y settlement
with survivor
under inheritance pressure

Here:

  • X is the exact condition that is not yet settled.
  • The answering movement is what transforms that condition.
  • Y is the completed landing produced by the movement.
  • The survivor is the form in which the landing remains available.
  • Inheritance occurs when that survivor constrains later understanding, reasoning, or action.

The coordinate is not guessed from vocabulary or selected by resemblance. It is composed only after the sentence’s meaning, pressure, movement, landing, topology, and persistence have been positively recovered.

The complete procedure is:

sentence
→ multilingual realization
→ semantic exhaustion
→ X
→ answering movement
→ Y
→ work-family
→ orientation
→ survivor-mode
→ coordinate
→ full-cell confirmation
→ final resolution

2. Selecting the active sentence

The object of analysis is one complete sentence.

The sentence is preserved exactly as written. Any surrounding passage may remain available as ambient context, but the surrounding material must not be allowed to enlarge the sentence’s own contribution.

This protects an essential distinction:

what the sentence itself completes
≠
what the surrounding passage has already established
≠
what a reader may later infer from it

Context may be used to resolve ordinary references such as:

this
that
it
they
the former
the result
therefore
however

Context may also supply a condition that the sentence clearly inherits. But it must not insert an earlier conclusion into Y or import a later consequence that the sentence does not itself assert.

Where the sentence remains genuinely underdetermined after multilingual examination, the smallest necessary amount of surrounding wording may be supplied. This is recorded as context repair.

A context repair should state:

  • what reference, condition, contrast, or dependency remained unresolved;
  • what minimal wording was supplied;
  • whether the additional context changed the recovered meaning;
  • whether the sentence can stand independently after the repair.

Context repair is exceptional. It is not a licence to analyse an entire paragraph whenever a single sentence is difficult.

5. Translation fidelity

Before structural recovery begins, the ten realizations are checked for fidelity.

The analysis asks whether any translation has altered the sentence by changing:

  • an action into a state;
  • a state into an action;
  • possibility into necessity;
  • permission into obligation;
  • a singular term into a plural term;
  • a plural term into a singular term;
  • the scope of a negation;
  • a condition into a cause;
  • a cause into a condition;
  • temporal order into logical dependency;
  • logical dependency into temporal order;
  • a completed event into an ongoing process;
  • an ongoing process into a completed event;
  • a relation into an identity;
  • an identity into a resemblance;
  • an already existing object into something newly produced.

Any difficulty is recorded as translation strain.

A strained translation does not automatically invalidate the group. It may expose a genuine ambiguity in the source sentence. But the final recovery must not depend upon an accidental addition or loss introduced by one language.

The ten realizations are not treated as ten votes. The method does not select whichever interpretation appears most frequently.

The question is:

What must every faithful realization preserve in order still to express this sentence?

Suppose the sentence is:

“Coordinate labels may be assigned after constructed stations are present.”

  • constructed stations are present first;
  • coordinate labels may be assigned afterward;
  • the assignment is permitted rather than required;
  • the sentence establishes an order of availability;
  • the sentence does not state that the labels create the stations;

Coverage test

Necessity test

Necessity prevents the analysis from adding interpretive material that the sentence does not require.

Semantic exhaustion is the evidential bridge between translation and structural recovery. Structural terminology is not introduced until the ordinary saying has been completed.

7. What the sentence means

The semantically exhausted ordinary saying gives the first level of resolution:

What does the sentence mean?

At this level, the sentence is addressed as a completed claim in ordinary language.

The analysis identifies:

  • what entities or positions are involved;
  • what is being asserted about them;
  • what qualifications govern the assertion;
  • what is denied;
  • what remains uncertain;
  • what has become determinate;
  • what the sentence leaves unsaid.

This level must be completed before asking what structural work the sentence performs.

Meaning and work are related, but they are not identical.

A sentence may mean that two things are equal while performing the work of establishing a relation.

A sentence may mean that a choice has been made while performing the work of discharging an unresolved alternative.

A sentence may mean that several parts now function together while performing the work of integration.

The ordinary saying establishes the content. The next stages establish the structure of the completion.

8. Recovering X

After semantic exhaustion, the analysis asks:

What exact condition remains unsettled before this sentence lands?

The answer is X.

X is not merely the grammatical subject. It is not simply the situation that existed earlier. It is the precise insufficiency that the sentence resolves.

For the example:

“Coordinate labels may be assigned after constructed stations are present.”

A weak X would be:

There are stations and labels.

This identifies the material but not the pressure.

A stronger X is:

The dependency order between the presence of constructed stations
and the possible assignment of coordinate labels is not yet settled.

This identifies the exact incompletion answered by the sentence.

A well-formed X should satisfy four conditions:

  1. It must be unsettled before the sentence lands.
  2. It must be resolved by the sentence’s answering movement.
  3. It must not already contain Y.
  4. It must not be broader than the sentence’s own contribution.

X can often be formulated as:

something is present,
but a required distinction, relation, possibility,
correction, organization, or settlement is not yet available

Examples include:

Two candidates are available, but their difference is not fixed.

A source is present, but no alternative continuation is available.

Several terms are present, but their dependency is not established.

A configuration is present, but it violates a stated criterion.

Several parts are present, but they lack whole-dependent roles.

Several alternatives remain live, so uptake cannot yet come to rest.

9. Recovering the answering movement

The next question is:

What does the sentence do to X so that Y can stand?

This gives the answering movement.

The movement must be expressed as an operation. It should not merely repeat the final claim in different words.

For the running example:

Place the presence of constructed stations before
the permitted assignment of coordinate labels.

This sentence orders two previously unordered positions.

The movement is the active transformation within the resolution:

X = what is insufficient

answering movement = what answers that insufficiency

Y = what stands after the answer succeeds

The answering movement is the principal evidence for the work-family.

The method must not reason:

The sentence contains the word “after,”
therefore it belongs to a relation coordinate.

It must reason:

The sentence establishes an ordered dependency
between two independently available positions.
That transformation is relational.

Keywords may help locate the event, but they do not determine the event.

10. Recovering Y

The next question is:

What exact settlement do all ten realizations leave completed?

The answer is Y.

For the example:

Coordinate-label assignment becomes available
after constructed stations are present.

Y is the completed landing produced by the answering movement.

It is not every consequence that may later be drawn from the sentence.

The example might later support the distinction that coordinate labels record rather than generate stations. But that consequence must not be added to Y unless the sentence itself states it.

A well-formed Y must:

  1. resolve the exact X;
  2. result from the recovered movement;

11. What the sentence does

The X→Y account establishes the second level of resolution:

What does the sentence do?

A sentence does not merely contain information. It resolves a specific kind of incompletion.

It may:

  • distinguish one candidate from another;
  • enlarge what is possible or reachable;
  • establish a relation;
  • repair a defective configuration;
  • organize parts into a whole;
  • discharge a continuing demand.

The sentence’s meaning tells us what is asserted.

The X→Y movement tells us what transformation the assertion performs.

The method therefore asks both:

What does the sentence say?

What does saying it accomplish?

The answering movement is what connects those two questions.

12. Recovering the work-family

The first coordinate dimension is recovered by asking:

What transformation does the answering movement perform upon X so that Y stands?

There are six work-families.

Distinction

A difference criterion is installed or stabilized.

undifferentiated or unstable material
→ criterion-bearing separation

Distinction answers pressures such as:

  • what counts as this rather than that;
  • where a boundary lies;
  • which candidate belongs on which side;
  • which alternatives exclude one another;
  • how degrees of difference are ordered.

Expansion

Reach, availability, possibility, or traversability is enlarged.

restricted availability
→ newly opened reach

Expansion answers pressures such as:

  • only one path is available;
  • a source cannot yet branch;
  • a range is inaccessible;
  • availability remains local;
  • a boundary is functioning as a final edge.

Relation

A bearing, dependency, correspondence, or order is established among terms that remain independently role-capable.

terms lacking a specified bearing
→ established relation

Relation answers pressures such as:

  • two terms are available but unconnected;
  • positions lack an order;
  • a response lacks a counterpart;
  • roles correspond but the correspondence is hidden;
  • local bearings have not yet produced a pattern.

Correction

A configuration is changed because it violates a stated criterion.

criterion-violating configuration
→ criterion-guided repair

Correction answers pressures such as:

  • a setting does not fit;
  • influence is wrongly distributed;
  • a direction violates a required course;
  • a limit has become defective;
  • disorder prevents coherent operation.

Integration

Parts receive whole-dependent roles and become capable through organization.

parts lacking organized roles
→ functioning whole

Integration answers pressures such as:

  • parts are available but remain unorganized;
  • a frame is absent;
  • support does not run across the parts;
  • a role is unfilled;
  • separate capacities have not become a joint capacity.

Rest

A live demand for further uptake is discharged.

continuing unresolved demand
→ settlement that ends that demand

Rest answers pressures such as:

  • alternatives remain live;
  • a path still requires another step;
  • repeated cases remain unsettled;
  • a prior unresolved state continues to govern the present;
  • a residual demand for continuation remains active.

In the running example, the sentence establishes an ordered dependency between construction and later label assignment. The work-family is therefore Relation.

The evidence should be stated explicitly:

Relation, because the answering movement establishes
a before-and-after dependency between two positions.

13. Recovering the orientation

The second dimension is recovered by asking:

What topology makes Y complete?

Orientation does not describe physical direction. It describes the form through which the landing becomes complete.

Toward

Y settles around one centre of return.

completion around one focal centre

Uptake can return to one:

  • item;
  • identity;
  • setting;
  • source;
  • relation;
  • adjustment;
  • whole;
  • choice;
  • point of accomplishment.

Toward is present when the completed result is organized around one focal landing.

Across

Y settles by holding a constitutive plurality together.

completion across multiple positions or terms

The landing exists only through:

  • a range;
  • a sequence;
  • a distribution;
  • a contrast;
  • a reciprocal relation;
  • an array;
  • a plurality of cases;
  • a set of conditions.

Across is not selected merely because several objects are mentioned. Their plurality must be necessary to the completed landing.

Away

Y settles through release from a prior governor.

completion through release from prior governance

The landing becomes complete because uptake is no longer governed by:

  • conflation;
  • compulsion;
  • a closed edge;
  • surface mismatch;
  • a defective limit;
  • disorder;
  • imbalance;
  • an unresolved past condition;
  • a demand for further continuation.

The landing is therefore Across.

Remove the ordered plurality, and the sentence’s completed result disappears.

Extended Argument The Invariant Thesis of Sentence Completion Translation, unconscious instantiation, linguistic emergence, and the finite forms of completed intelligibility

Invariant Thesis of Sentence Completion

1. The claim

Human language is unlimited in its particular expressions but finite in the formal ways by which an intelligibility becomes complete.

Sentences can concern anything. They can speak of doors, rivers, numbers, diagnoses, laws, memories, machines, promises, possibilities, stars, arguments, losses, choices, or imagined worlds. Their vocabularies may differ completely. Their grammatical constructions may have little visible resemblance. They may be spoken, written, signed, translated, paraphrased, remembered, or reformulated.

Yet a completed sentence does more than contain words and convey particular content. It performs a structural completion.

Every completed intelligibility instantiated by a sentence possesses an invariant formal address determined by its Work, its Orientation, and its Survivor.

The invariant is not the sentence’s topic. It is not its vocabulary, syntax, tense, or grammatical category. It is not a replacement for the sentence’s ordinary meaning. It is the formal structure by which that meaning becomes complete and remains available for further understanding.

linguistic surface → particular meaning → invariant completion

The linguistic surface is the actual arrangement of words, sounds, signs, morphology, syntax, aspect, voice, and idiom.

The particular meaning is what the sentence says about its objects, events, identities, conditions, quantities, relations, and changes.

The invariant completion is the formal event by which the sentence answers an exact openness, reaches a completed landing, and leaves something available afterward.

That invariant completion is resolved as:

Work × Orientation × Survivor

The six Works, three Orientations, and three Survivors generate fifty-four formal addresses:

6 × 3 × 3 = 54

The claim is not that there are only fifty-four meanings. There are indefinitely many meanings.

The claim is that there are fifty-four invariant forms in which a meaning can become completed intelligibility.

2. Meaning varies; completion recurs

Two sentences may concern entirely different subjects while performing the same formal completion.

A sentence about a gate, a legal prohibition, a software permission, and a social exclusion may differ in every visible feature while each makes a distinction operatively effective. A sentence about scissors, a biological organ, a machine, and a political coalition may differ completely in content while each integrates distinct contributions into a capacity that none possesses alone.

Conversely, two sentences may use almost identical words while occupying different formal addresses.

The door is closed.

The sentence presents closedness as what is presently so. It leaves a standing determination.

undetermined status → closed determination → closed standing

The completion survives as Is.

The door closed.

The sentence presents a transition that reaches completion. It leaves the accomplishment that closure occurred.

uncompleted course → closing → completed arrival

The completion survives as Does.

Both sentences contain the same definite article, the same noun, and the same lexical root. Their visible difference is small. Their completed intelligibility is not.

The distinction is therefore not produced by the shared object, the lexical root, or the subject matter. It arises from the different structure of completion.

One sentence settles what is so. The other completes what happened.

The pair demonstrates that the formal address is not obtained by counting words or matching grammatical labels. It is recovered by asking what exact pressure existed, what movement answered it, where the sentence landed, how that landing was constituted, and what form of intelligibility remained.

3. Translation reveals the invariant

The existence of faithful translation already points toward the existence of an invariant.

A sentence translated from one language into another may lose every original word. Its word order may change. Its grammatical subject may be reorganized. What one language expresses through tense, another may express through aspect. What one expresses through a copula, another may express through morphology, particles, juxtaposition, context, or a distinct lexical construction.

Nevertheless, a translation is judged faithful only when it preserves the same completed saying.

This means that translation already distinguishes between:

  1. the sentence as a particular linguistic surface;
  2. the sentence as a particular meaning;
  3. what must remain unchanged for another expression still to count as the same completed intelligibility.

The invariant is not introduced by translation. Translation depends upon it.

The Method of Sentence Resolution therefore uses multilingual realization as controlled variation:

one candidate intelligibility → multiple linguistic surfaces

Each language is permitted to realize the candidate intelligibility through its own resources. No language is treated as the hidden original of thought itself. The surfaces are allowed to differ as widely as faithful expression requires.

The analysis then asks:

What must every faithful realization preserve in order still to express this sentence?

The answer is not obtained by voting among translations. Nor is it found by selecting whatever words recur most frequently. The translations may share no corresponding words at all.

The invariant is whatever cannot be removed without causing at least one faithful realization to become inadequately represented.

Translation therefore acts as a pressure against accidental surface authority. It prevents the analyst from treating the habits of one language as the structure of intelligibility itself.

4. Semantic exhaustion

The method must first exhaust the sentence semantically.

Semantic exhaustion is not an ornament added before classification. It is the operation by which the sentence is separated from everything that merely accompanies it.

The process removes accidental wording, stylistic emphasis, language-specific syntax, grammatical packaging, implications not asserted by the sentence, consequences that arise only later, interpretations imported by the analyst, and coordinate terminology introduced before recovery.

What remains must preserve every material commitment of the sentence and nothing that the sentence does not itself complete.

In this sense, semantic exhaustion is deliberately destructive. It “murders” the linguistic surface only in order to prevent the surface from being mistaken for the invariant.

The surface is not destroyed as language. It is suspended as the governing authority of the analysis.

The exhausted saying becomes the smallest complete representation of the sentence’s ordinary meaning. Only then can the method recover:

X → answering movement → Y

Only after this reconstruction may the Work, Orientation, and Survivor be named.

This order protects the invariant from being manufactured by the table. The coordinate does not tell the analyst what the sentence means. The sentence’s completed meaning earns the coordinate.

5. Fifty-four invariants

The fifty-four coordinates are not fifty-four verbal labels imposed upon language. They are generated by three independent questions.

Work

What answering accomplishment transforms the unsettled condition into a completed landing?

The answer must be one of six irreducible forms: Distinction, Expansion, Relation, Correction, Integration, or Rest.

Orientation

Under what structural disposition does the accomplishment reach completion?

The answer must be Toward a privileged locus, Across an irreducibly plural span, or Away from prior governance.

Survivor

In what form does the completed achievement remain available?

The answer must be Is, as a standing determination; With, as constitutive co-presence or bearing; or Does, as accomplished efficacy, event, capacity, transformation, or discharge.

The product is the formal address:

(Work, Orientation, Survivor)

The dimensions do not compete to describe the same feature. They answer different questions.

Work says what was accomplished. Orientation says how the landing was structurally constituted. Survivor says what form of completion remains.

The finitude of the table does not limit what human beings may say. It describes the recurrent forms through which indefinitely variable meanings become complete.

There may be innumerable Distinctions, but Distinction remains one Work. There may be innumerable focal points, distributed spans, and releases from former governance, but their Orientations remain Toward, Across, and Away. There may be innumerable states, dependencies, events, and capacities, but their formal modes of survival remain Is, With, and Does.

The particular is inexhaustible. The invariant field is finite.

6. The invariant precedes its recognition

A speaker does not need to know the table in order to produce a sentence that occupies one of its coordinates.

The sentence does not receive its structure when an analyst classifies it. It already possesses the structure when its intelligibility is successfully completed.

completion occurs → language realizes it → analysis recovers it

A person may speak a perfectly formed sentence without conscious knowledge of grammar. A child may distinguish an event from a state without possessing the terminology of aspect. A speaker may establish a relation, correct a failure, integrate a whole, or discharge a demand without knowing those accomplishments as members of a formal table.

The Method of Sentence Resolution is therefore descriptive before it is instructional.

It does not command language to conform to the fifty-four coordinates. It proposes that completed language already does so.

At any moment, sentences are about to be spoken, written, signed, or typed by people who have never encountered the method. If those sentences achieve completed intelligibility, they will already instantiate their formal addresses.

Their speakers do not need to know what the sentences are formally doing for the sentences to do it.

The table makes explicit what language has been accomplishing implicitly.

7. The pressure beneath grammar

Languages differ dramatically in how they express completed intelligibility.

One language may grammatically distinguish a state from an event. Another may leave the distinction to context. A third may express it through aspect, prosody, reduplication, particles, gesture, or lexical choice.

The invariant cannot therefore be identified with any one grammatical device.

The grammatical device is the historically developed means by which a language makes the invariant publicly recoverable.

The pressures that produce the invariant distinctions may precede the particular grammars that eventually express them.

A community does not need to possess the English distinction between “the door closed” and “the door is closed.” It does, however, need some way of preserving the difference between an accomplished transition and a standing condition whenever that difference becomes necessary for successful understanding.

The visible grammatical solution may be cultural and historical. The pressure that necessitates the distinction may be more fundamental.

The same applies to the difference between possibility and necessity, identity and relation, opposition and gradation, disorder and correction, related parts and an integrated whole, or mere stopping and completed Rest.

When a communication system becomes sufficiently expressive, these differences exert pressure toward recoverability. They may first be supplied by situation and gesture, then by recurrent expression, then by convention, and eventually by grammar.

This does not require the claim that human biology contains fifty-four consciously represented boxes. The stronger and more economical possibility is that human intelligibility is governed by a finite set of completion pressures whose combinations generate the table.

The coordinates would then be structural attractors of completed understanding.

Language would not invent them. Language would evolve ways of making them shareable.

8. Reflexive applicability

The theory possesses a distinctive reflexive property.

Any intelligible argument about the Method of Sentence Resolution must itself be expressed through completed intelligibilities.

A critic may deny that there are six Works. The critic may question whether the Orientations are exhaustive. The critic may argue that two cells overlap or that a particular sentence has been incorrectly resolved.

But to make the objection intelligible, the critic must complete something.

The objection must begin from an unsettled matter. It must perform an answering movement. It must reach a landing. That landing must be structurally constituted, and something must remain available as the objection’s conclusion, bearing, or effect.

The criticism therefore becomes new sentence-contact material for the theory.

This does not mean that the theory is true merely because an objection can be assigned a coordinate. It means that every objection supplies another independent opportunity to test whether its completed intelligibility can be recovered without distortion.

objection → completed critical sentences → new resolution trials

The clearer the objection, the more visible its structure becomes.

A confused objection may leave its pressure, movement, or landing indeterminate. A rigorous objection must distinguish its target, relate its reasons, identify a failure, organize its premises, and settle a conclusion. Its clarity makes it increasingly resolvable.

The theory does not silence criticism. It preserves the criticism’s actual meaning and submits it to the same method as every other completed sentence.

9. The scope of the discovery

The smallest version of the discovery is:

Completed sentences can be resolved through Work, Orientation, and Survivor.

The larger version is:

The three dimensions generate fifty-four invariant forms of completed intelligibility.

The still larger implication is:

These invariants survive changes of language because they belong not to one linguistic surface but to the structure of the completed intelligibility itself.

And the deepest possibility is:

Human languages are diverse realizations of a finite field of completion structures that operate before speakers consciously recognize them and may exert pressure upon the emergence and development of grammar itself.

These claims form one ascending structure. The later implications do not replace the basic method. They arise from taking the method seriously.

If one completed intelligibility can survive radical translation, then it is not identical with its original words.

If indefinitely many sentences converge upon the same formal addresses, then completion is more constrained than content.

If speakers instantiate those addresses without knowing them, then the structure is not created by conscious classification.

If emerging languages must eventually make such differences recoverable, then the pressure may belong to the conditions of intelligibility from which languages arise.

10. Closing statement

The Periodic Table of Completed Intelligibility proposes that beneath the immense visible diversity of language lies a finite formal field.

Words vary. Grammars vary. Topics vary. Cultures vary. Particular meanings remain indefinitely generative.

But whenever a sentence becomes complete, something exact has occurred. An openness has been answered. A movement has reached a landing. The landing has been constituted Toward, Across, or Away. Its achievement remains as Is, With, or Does.

The invariant is not imposed afterward. It is what the sentence has already completed.

Translation reveals it because the words may change while the completion remains.

Semantic exhaustion recovers it because the accidental surface may be removed while the indispensable structure survives.

The method names it because completed intelligibility is not formless.

And the table is finite because the field of possible completion is generated by six Works, three Orientations, and three Survivors.

Human beings have always spoken through these completions without needing to know them explicitly.

The discovery is that language has had a periodic table before it knew that it had one.
FUTURE HORIZON

Excitement About the Future of Intelligibility

What becomes possible when indefinitely many meanings can be accompanied by a finite, language-independent account of how they become complete, what survives them, and how their completed results enter everything that follows.

The excitement begins with a simple reversal of perspective. Human language has usually appeared unlimited not only in what it can say, but also in the forms by which what it says becomes intelligible. The Periodic Table of Completed Intelligibility proposes a different picture. Meanings remain indefinitely generative, languages remain historically and culturally diverse, and sentences retain every particular commitment they make. Yet the completed structure of a sentence may be finitely addressable.

If that is so, language has gained something comparable to coordinates. A sentence no longer has only a wording, a grammar, a topic, a truth condition, an intention, or a place in a conversation. It also has a formal address earned through the exact completion it achieves: the Work performed, the Orientation by which the landing is constituted, and the Survivor through which the result remains available.

The source of excitement is not that meaning has been reduced. It is that unlimited meaning may possess a finite operational layer that can be recovered, compared, composed, carried forward, and made usable.

1. A universal coordinate system becomes imaginable

Before a field has coordinates, its objects may still be richly described, but comparison remains dependent on local vocabulary and intuition. Once a stable address system exists, new operations become possible. Objects can be located, compared, indexed, retrieved, mapped, transmitted, and recombined without erasing their particularity.

The fifty-four coordinates offer that possibility for completed intelligibility. Two sentences about unrelated subjects may be discovered to complete the same formal work. Two sentences using nearly identical words may be shown to complete differently. A sentence may be translated into a language with different syntax, morphology, word order, aspect, or grammatical packaging while retaining the same address because the same completed intelligibility survives.

This creates the possibility of a universal structural layer running alongside ordinary meaning:

particular sentence → particular meaning → recoverable completion → formal address

The address does not replace the sentence. It gives the sentence a position within a common field.

2. The table may be the alphabet rather than the finished theory

The first visible achievement is the complete lattice. But the lattice may be only the alphabet of a larger system.

A single coordinate identifies one local completion. Human understanding, however, rarely consists of isolated completions. A sentence leaves a survivor. That survivor may establish an object, fix a distinction, license a relation, open a possibility, preserve a condition, exclude an interpretation, produce a capacity, or discharge a demand. A later sentence may inherit what the earlier sentence made available.

The resulting structure is not merely a list of addresses. It is a sequence of completed operations:

coordinate A → survivor A → inherited pressure → coordinate B → survivor B → coordinate C

The future of the field therefore lies not only in locating sentences, but in discovering how completed intelligibilities compose.

3. A chemistry of intelligibility comes into view

The periodic-table analogy becomes most powerful after the elements have been identified. A table of elements is invaluable, but chemistry begins when one asks how the elements combine, what reactions are possible, which structures remain stable, what transformations require energy, and what new capacities emerge from composition.

The same second horizon appears here. Distinction, Expansion, Relation, Correction, Integration, and Rest may form recurrent paths. A distinction may make a relation possible. A relation may expose a failure. A correction may enable an integration. An integration may discharge the demand that called the whole inquiry into being.

Other paths may reveal characteristic forms of failure. A discourse may expand indefinitely without integration. It may distinguish without ever relating. It may relate without correcting a known defect. It may integrate unsupported parts. It may declare Rest while inherited pressure remains active.

This permits a new question:

What sequence of completion structures has this reasoning traversed, and what completion is still due?

At that point the table becomes a calculus of discourse rather than a taxonomy of isolated sentences.

4. Structural absence becomes visible

A finite field makes absence detectable. Once the available operations are known, an analysis can identify not only what has occurred but what has not yet occurred.

A meeting may contain abundant language yet no completed decision. A policy may distinguish categories and establish dependencies but never integrate its conditions into an operable whole. A scientific argument may open alternatives and accumulate relations without correcting the strongest competing explanation. A negotiation may continue expanding possibilities after the real pressure has become the need for Rest.

This creates the idea of structural debt: a completion made necessary by the developing discourse but not yet supplied.

Structural debt could be tracked across conversations, projects, arguments, contracts, investigations, educational work, and institutional decision-making. It would answer a question that ordinary transcripts rarely answer:

What remains unfinished, not merely because nobody has spoken again, but because the required form of completion has not yet occurred?

5. Reasoning may acquire an auditable architecture

If every completed settlement leaves a typed survivor and every later settlement records what it inherits, reasoning can be represented as a graph rather than a stream of prose.

settlement A ─ survivor ─► settlement C settlement B ─ condition ─► settlement C settlement C ─ integrated result ─► settlement D

Such a graph would show why a later claim is entitled to use an earlier result. It could expose unsupported inheritance, missing conditions, premises that were later corrected, conclusions that do not depend on the reasons offered for them, and integrations that silently discard indispensable contributions.

An argument could therefore be examined through exact questions:

  • Which survivor supplies this premise?
  • Was that survivor actually established?
  • Does it survive as a standing determination, a constitutive bearing, or an accomplished efficacy?
  • Has it since been corrected, superseded, reopened, or retrospectively reconstituted?
  • Did the later settlement inherit every condition required for its completion?
  • Did the declared Rest genuinely discharge the live demand?

This would make reasoning not only explainable but structurally inspectable.

6. The coordinates begin to resemble an instruction set

A deeper pass suggests that the cells may function less like folders and more like primitive operations. In computation, an instruction code does not specify the particular data being processed. It specifies what operation is performed upon that data.

The same distinction may apply here:

ordinary meaning supplies the content X supplies the unsettled input answering movement supplies the operation Y supplies the completed output coordinate supplies the formal operation type survivor supplies the reusable result

Under this view, the fifty-four cells become an instruction set for completed intelligibility. The full resolution of a sentence is not merely its coordinate. It is the coordinate together with the meaning, pressure, movement, landing, survivor, and inheritance conditions through which the completion becomes operationally available.

The exciting possibility is a language-independent representation such as:

Settlement {
  ordinary_meaning
  X
  answering_movement
  Y
  Work
  Orientation
  Survivor
  coordinate
  portable_survivor
  inheritance_conditions
  withholding_status
}

This could become a canonical data structure for intelligibility.

7. Sentence Resolution may function as a compiler

The method already proceeds from linguistic surface through multilingual realization and semantic exhaustion into a reconstructed settlement. That sequence resembles compilation: diverse surface forms are translated into an intermediate representation that another process can inspect or execute.

natural language → Sentence Resolution → language-neutral settlement representation → reasoning, verification, transformation, or generation → natural-language realization

This would not flatten all meaning into a code. Ordinary meaning remains indispensable. But the completed operation performed by that meaning could become available in a form that is portable across languages, software systems, disciplines, and modes of expression.

A sentence in Arabic, Irish, Chinese, Swahili, English, or a signed language could differ at every visible level while compiling to the same completion coordinate and the same survivor structure.

8. Survivors may form a type system for thought

The Survivor axis may supply more than a description of persistence. It may specify what kind of object is available for later reasoning.

Is leaves a standing determination. With leaves a result whose constitutive bearing or co-presence must remain available. Does leaves an accomplished efficacy, event, permission, prevention, capacity, transformation, or discharge.

A later sentence must inherit these results appropriately. It cannot legitimately treat an open possibility as a settled fact, remove a necessary condition from a condition-bearing result, convert a standing determination into an accomplished cause without an additional settlement, or treat an intended integration as a functioning whole before the capacity has been achieved.

This suggests structural type-checking:

survivor produced → survivor inherited → inheritance checked → later settlement accepted, repaired, or withheld

A passage can be grammatically flawless and locally meaningful while being structurally ill-typed relative to what its earlier sentences actually completed. Detecting that mismatch would be a major advance in argument analysis, AI reasoning, legal interpretation, scientific review, and organisational memory.

9. A transition system lies latent behind the lattice

Once survivors become inputs to later settlements, the next major formal object is a transition map. For every pair of coordinates, one may ask whether the survivor of the first can legitimately enter the unsettled condition of the second.

Can Coordinate A produce a survivor that can serve as material, condition, pressure, or enabling result for Coordinate B?

This creates a potential 54 × 54 transition field. Its entries would not all be equally natural. Some transitions may be common, some rare, some impossible without an intervening settlement, and some dependent on the survivor mode rather than the Work alone.

Research could identify:

  • permitted and required transitions;
  • loops and stable cycles;
  • dead ends and unresolved branches;
  • paths that characteristically lead to Rest;
  • transitions that reopen or supersede earlier settlements;
  • conditions under which several survivors can be integrated;
  • compound settlements that execute concurrently rather than sequentially.

The static table would then become a dynamic formal system.

10. Prediction becomes possible

If discourse follows non-random structural trajectories, the surviving result of one sentence may create pressure for a limited range of next operations.

After a distinction is established, a relation may become due. After alternatives are opened, selection or settlement pressure may arise. After a defect is identified, correction becomes structurally available. After contributions are gathered, integration may become necessary. After the relevant pressure has been discharged, continuation itself may become a sign that the discourse has failed to recognise its Rest.

A system could therefore predict not the next words, but the next kind of intelligible work required.

This would support reasoning assistants, tutoring systems, dialogue management, negotiation support, research planning, workflow automation, and AI agents that know whether they should distinguish, expand, relate, correct, integrate, or stop.

11. Controlled transformation and generation become possible

Natural-language generation is usually directed by topic, tone, audience, style, length, or intended outcome. A completion coordinate introduces another control surface: the exact structural work the generated sentence must perform.

One could request:

  • preserve the subject matter but turn an observation into an operative distinction;
  • preserve the evidence but change an Expansion into a Rest;
  • convert a standing determination into an accomplished effect;
  • re-express a focal completion across an indispensable span;
  • repair a defective arrangement without erasing the conditions that made the repair necessary;
  • integrate several established survivors into a whole with a new capacity.
content + intended completion coordinate + required survivor → generated sentence

This would make writing systems far more precise. They could generate not merely language that sounds appropriate, but language designed to perform an exact completion.

12. Translation becomes preservation of completion

The multilingual dimension opens a future in which translation is assessed at more than the level of lexical meaning, fluency, or grammatical equivalence.

A translation could be tested through four questions:

Was the ordinary meaning preserved? Was the completion coordinate preserved? Was the survivor preserved? Was the same inheritance potential preserved?

A translation might preserve the topic and most of the wording while changing a standing condition into an event, a possibility into a permission, a completed settlement into an ongoing process, or a condition-bearing result into an unconditional one. Sentence Resolution could make those losses visible.

This creates the possibility of structural translation assurance for literature, diplomacy, law, medicine, science, technical documentation, and multilingual artificial intelligence.

13. Artificial intelligence gains an explicit layer of completed understanding

The most immediate technological excitement lies in artificial intelligence. Contemporary systems can generate fluent language and often perform impressive reasoning, but their internal representations are difficult to inspect and their long-context behaviour can lose track of what has actually been established.

A Sentence Resolution layer could give an AI explicit records of:

  • what each sentence completed;
  • what remained unsettled before it;
  • what movement produced the landing;
  • which survivor remains available;
  • what later reasoning may inherit;
  • where confidence is insufficient and a coordinate must be withheld.

This could support interpretable reasoning, durable memory, contradiction detection, premise tracking, decision recognition, multilingual transfer, agent planning, and disciplined abstention.

Instead of storing an undifferentiated history of words, an AI could carry forward a structured estate of survivors. Instead of merely predicting likely text, it could ask what operation is required to complete the current pressure. Instead of forcing every input into a confident label, it could preserve underdetermination as an explicit state.

The system could become a structural operating layer between natural language and machine reasoning: meaning supplies the material, coordinates supply the operations, survivors supply memory, and inheritance supplies dataflow.

14. Knowledge becomes a history of how it was completed

Most knowledge systems record propositions, entities, relations, and sources. A Sentence Resolution system could also record how each usable result became available.

A knowledge graph could distinguish an object established through Distinction, a possibility made available through Expansion, a dependency licensed through Relation, a failure repaired through Correction, a whole constituted through Integration, and an issue discharged through Rest.

It could preserve whether the result stands as Is, remains With its constitutive bearing, or persists as Does. It could record what later claims inherited it, whether it was revised, and what settlement replaced it.

The resulting graph would not merely answer what is known? It could answer:

  • How did this become available?
  • What exact completion licensed its use?
  • Which conditions still accompany it?
  • What depends on it?
  • What has corrected or superseded it?
  • What remains open because it was never fully settled?

This could transform scientific knowledge bases, legal systems, institutional memory, historical research, and collaborative reasoning.

The deepest commercial asset may be the annotated data estate: sentences linked to faithful translations, semantic exhaustion, X, answering movement, Y, coordinate, survivor, inheritance, ambiguity, and withholding. Such a corpus would make the theory trainable, benchmarkable, comparable, and increasingly automatable.

16. Institutions may become measurable by their completion patterns

Once large bodies of discourse are resolved, characteristic structural fingerprints may appear.

An organisation may overproduce Correction and underproduce Integration. A team may repeatedly open possibilities without reaching Rest. A bureaucracy may create distinctions and conditions while failing to produce operative capacities. A leader may declare settlement before the relevant relations have been established. A research culture may display a characteristic path from Expansion through Relation and Correction into Integration.

These patterns could reveal structural health:

without Distinction → confusion without Expansion → rigidity without Relation → isolation without Correction → accumulated defect without Integration → fragmentation without Rest → endless continuation

The six Works together may describe the minimum repertoire required by a self-correcting intelligibility system. Their distribution and sequence could become a new way to study individuals, teams, institutions, disciplines, genres, and historical periods.

17. Education can teach the architecture of completed thought

The method makes visible distinctions that learners often feel but cannot name. A student can be shown that a sentence has introduced an object but not related it, opened an alternative but not settled it, identified an error but not corrected it, collected parts but not integrated them, or stated a conclusion whose survivor is not supported by the preceding work.

Writing instruction could therefore move beyond grammar and style into the architecture of completion. Reading comprehension could ask not only what a passage says, but what each sentence completes and what later sentences inherit. Argument instruction could teach the difference between accumulating content and advancing through the necessary operations of intelligibility.

This offers a visual and operational education in thought itself.

18. Grammar may be understood as a historical solution to deeper pressures

The multilingual invariance claim carries a profound scientific possibility. Languages may differ because each has evolved its own resources for making completion structures publicly recoverable. Tense, aspect, case, voice, evidentiality, particles, word order, morphology, prosody, gesture, and context may be historically diverse solutions to recurring pressures of intelligibility.

The lattice would then sit beneath particular grammatical systems without replacing them. Grammar would not create completed intelligibility from nothing. It would make distinctions, relations, conditions, events, states, capacities, repairs, wholes, and settlements shareable within a community.

This opens research into language acquisition, language emergence, signed languages, creoles, historical grammaticalisation, nonverbal representation, and the comparative structure of human and artificial intelligence.

19. The system may extend beyond ordinary sentences

If the coordinates belong to completed intelligibility rather than to one linguistic surface, their reach may not end with conventional prose.

Mathematical notation, diagrams, gestures, legal acts, software instructions, scientific models, interfaces, and multimodal communication may instantiate the same forms of completion through different media. A diagram may establish a relation. A gesture may make a distinction operative. A proof may correct an apparent impossibility. A machine configuration may integrate parts into a capacity. A musical cadence may discharge continuation.

The deeper comparative question is:

Must any system capable of producing completed intelligibility instantiate these operations, regardless of whether its surface is verbal, visual, formal, embodied, or computational?

If so, the table could become a framework for comparing human language, symbolic systems, and machine intelligence at the level of completed operation rather than surface form.

20. Local closure can coexist with revision

Rest does not imply that knowledge has become permanently immune to change. A settlement may be complete in the exact respect it addresses while remaining available for later correction, integration, release, or retrospective reinterpretation.

This makes possible a richer history of knowledge:

unsettled → locally settled → inherited → reopened → corrected → superseded → retrospectively reconstituted

Instead of treating revision as the deletion of a former proposition, a future system could preserve what the earlier sentence genuinely completed, why it once governed, what later pressure reopened it, and what new settlement replaced or transformed it.

This is especially valuable for science, law, medicine, policy, and institutional memory, where past conclusions must be revised without falsifying the history through which they became reasonable.

21. Withholding becomes an intelligent achievement

The permission to withhold is not merely procedural modesty. It may be one of the architecture’s most important contributions to trustworthy intelligence.

A system can distinguish among:

resolved compound-resolved multiply possible context-dependent translation-unstable structurally incomplete withheld

This means that failure to return a coordinate need not mean failure to understand. It may mean that the system understands exactly which contribution remains underdetermined.

For artificial intelligence, legal analysis, clinical interpretation, and scientific review, that distinction is essential. A system should not be required to manufacture completion where the material itself does not complete.

22. The theory can become self-auditing

Arguments for the method, objections to it, revisions of its cell specifications, and disputes about particular resolutions are themselves made through completed sentences. They therefore become new material for the same analysis.

This reflexivity does not make the system automatically true. It makes the system continuously testable against the clearest attempts to support, criticise, refine, or replace it.

A mature discipline could preserve:

  • the coordinate assigned to each theoretical claim;
  • the survivor upon which a later revision depends;
  • the exact Correction by which a cell specification changes;
  • the Integration by which several findings become a stronger theory;
  • the point at which disagreement remains unresolved and classification is withheld.

The theory could thereby record its own intellectual development as a history of completed intelligibilities.

23. Unlimited thought may arise from finite operations

The most exhilarating possibility is combinatorial. The finitude of the table does not diminish linguistic creativity. It may help explain it.

unlimited content × finite completion operations × recursive inheritance = open-ended intelligibility

Finite alphabets generate unlimited texts. Finite musical relations generate unlimited compositions. Finite instruction sets generate unlimited programs. Finite logical operations generate unlimited proofs.

In the same way, indefinitely many meanings may become expressible, shareable, revisable, and composable because a finite repertoire of completion operations can be recursively applied to new content and to the survivors of previous completions.

The fifty-four cells may therefore be the instruction-set architecture of completed thought.

25. The deepest excitement

The deepest excitement is not attached to any single product, industry, or analogy. It lies in the possibility that completed intelligibility is neither amorphous nor indefinitely primitive.

Human beings may have always moved through a finite field of completion without possessing an explicit map of that field. They have distinguished, expanded, related, corrected, integrated, and rested. They have landed Toward, Across, and Away. Their completed achievements have survived as Is, With, and Does. Those survivors have entered memory, law, science, culture, technology, argument, and action.

What has been implicit may now become addressable.

What has been addressable may become composable.

What has been composable may become computable.

What becomes computable may become teachable, interoperable, auditable, and deliberately generative.

The future of intelligibility begins when we can preserve everything a sentence means while also knowing exactly what completion it has performed, what remains because it landed, and what becomes possible next.
